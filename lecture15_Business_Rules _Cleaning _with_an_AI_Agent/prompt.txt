   column IS_RETURN, and add ABS_QUANTITY as the absolute value.

4. Prices: cast to NUMBER(10,2). Blank or 0 unit_price -> NULL, not zero.
5. Categories: title case, so 'beauty' and 'Beauty' become 'Beauty'.
6. Add LINE_REVENUE to STG_ORDER_ITEMS = quantity * unit_price * (1 - discount_pct/100).
